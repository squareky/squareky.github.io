Dr. Fangtian Zhong (钟方天)

Faculty member

School of Computer Science & Technology, Shandong University (山东大学)

Dr. Fangtian Zhong's research focuses on Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Program Analysis, and AI/ML for Software Security. He enjoys developing statistically and mathematically elegant solutions to software security challenges across various architectures, with a particular focus on native code analysis without source access.

Email Google Scholar GitHub Publications

Biography & Background

Dr. Fangtian Zhong is currently a faculty member at Shandong University (山东大学). He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from The George Washington University in 2021. From 2021 to 2023, he worked as a postdoctoral scholar at The Pennsylvania State University (Penn State) and the University of Notre Dame. Previously, he served as an Assistant Professor at Montana State University.

He has published two ESI 1% highly cited papers and received the Best Paper Award at IEEE CARS 2024 and Best Poster Award at IEEE S&P 2025. Additionally, he serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM) and IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (TII).
