About Me

I am an Assistant Professor leading the Intelligent Software Systems Lab (ISSL). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar at MIT CSAIL advised by Prof. Jane Doe, and completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University.

My research focuses on designing formal program analysis tools and leveraging Machine Learning for Code (Code ML) to make software systems safer, faster, and more maintainable. My lab works closely with industry collaborators to translate research prototypes into real-world developer tools.
